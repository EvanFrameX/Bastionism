# Idealimpero

If you have no idea where to put yourself in the vast world of politics, then you can consider reading to the end.

---

## 1. Communities

Society splits into communities based on ideology (leftists with leftists, rightists with rightists, centrists with centrists, etcetera) to reduces internal conflict.

If your views change, you can move to another community (or out of the state). If you cause major disruption, you will be forced to leave the community.

---

## 2. Leadership

Each community chooses either a single Democratic Dictator (Rodangher) or in a group. The candidates are selected based on:

- Past work output (PWO) — those who contributed more in the past are considered first
- Physical capability — leadership includes military responsibility

If multiple candidates exist and the community wants a Rodangher, there is a vote. If the community prefers collective leadership, then a party is formed.

Leaders defend the community and serve in the national military. Their decisions over their community must be account for democratic input (do their choices based on the democratic vote) and economic consequences (inflation, unfairness, harm to the community).

The term for the community in Bastionist terms is **Kazter**

If a leader fails or loses trust, the community can overthrow them through protests. After removal, the community will elect a new leader

---

## 3. Economy

Bastionism operates on socialist distribution: everyone works based on ability and each receives based on need.

Extra work is recognized. If the community has surplus, harder workers choose to either receive better quality goods or a currency that can expire but can be used as money. If surplus is not available, they receive public recognition. This system is called **Stokhaned** (name is inspired by the Soviet ***Aleksej Stakhanov***) 

Communities share/trade resources with each other when needed. Complete autarky is not required; Trade with other nations is permitted when necessary, but must be democratically approved by whatever community is affected.

---

## 4. National Government

The national Bastionist Party exists to:

- Assign jobs based on individual ability
- Distribute resources fairly based on need
- Reward harder workers where possible (Stokhaned)

Major decisions (war, foreign policy, outside trading) are made collectively by all community leaders or only those directly affected. No single leader decides for the whole, preventing rebellion.

Even though the military is mainly made out of the leaders of the communities, members who don't have education or experience can take a role in the national military. In case the military doesn't fit the member, simple community jobs (cashier, waiter, etcetera) are open.

Similarly, in case a member can't work for the community, they can choose to either have the needed medical help to continue working or take a job online (programmer, gamedev, software developer, researcher, etc...)

---

## 5. Rights

You have freedom of speech, press, and privacy as long as you do not harm the community nor the state as a whole.

Insulting a leader is not harm unless it becomes persistent or destabilizing. If you become annoying to the majority or to leadership itself, you will be expelled from the community.

There is no right to disrupt functional organization.

**The only restrictions** include anything related to racism (including robophobia), sexism, anti-semitism, transphobia, homophobia, or anything that makes some people look weaker or inferior. Punishment depends on the Rodangher's/BDF's authoritarian choice (no democracy needed)

---

## 6. Separation in singular ideologies

These are in case you agree with Bastionism but not all (not considered a Bastionist if you agree with less than half)

**Stokhanevism** — The idea that Socialism works better when the quality of the resources you get is higher as you put more effort in the contribution of your community/state.

**Social Nationalism** — The idea that Autarky works but trading with outside countries is still a good thing in times of crisis or potential upgrade in the state

**Federal Stratocracy** — The idea that the state should be devided into communities ruled by 1 or more people in the military

**Proto-Oligarchism** — The idea that the leader of a community or state should be choosen based on the effort and contribution put in the past years

**Rodangherism** — The idea that the government of a community or state must be 100% democratic

**Expulsionism** — The idea that, instead of putting criminals in cells or execute them, they get banned from their community and, in more severe cases, from the state.

**Teleproductivism** — The idea that, if someone is disabled in ways that make it difficult to work with others (e.g. Farmer, Doctor, Miner, etc...), they can work on internet-based jobs (e.g. Softwate Developing, Game Developing, etc...) 

---

## 7. Goal

Keep the state untouched; fight foreign imperialism; maintain a strong, functional country.

Not propaganda or moral perfection, just defense and stability.

---

## 8. Synarchism (EXTRA)

This 8th section documents another of the forms of Bastionism, influenced by the theory and ideologies of [Xeroise Ronango'O](https://stoneworksmc.fandom.com/wiki/Xeroise_Ronango%27O) and her ideology, Xeroism

The first variant, born from the consideration of Xeroist ideas, is **Ortharchism**, which says that Xeroism works best if mixed with Bastionist principles. The only practical change from classical Xeroism is that the goals change from **achieving a Communist state** to **keeping the state strong and fight external imperialism**.

The second variant, born from the **nostalgia of past empires** (E.g. Roman Empire) is **Baliearchism**, which is uncommon btu still possible. Baliearchism says that Ortharchism works and should rule over a vast empire, therefore it should expand its influence

The third variant, born from the ideal that Baliearchism will eventually run out of land to expand its influence, is **Autokratorism**, which is established right after a Baliearchist state stops on the Imperialist expansion of territory


Now, I expected some questions deriving from the creation of these 3 variants. So I'll answer them to be clear

**Why would I make these ideologies if Bastionism is still my main one?** - There are 2 reasons for this one. The reactions from some right-wing people wasn't very pro-Bastionist, even trying to moderate left-wing and right-wing to make a state that is acceptable, efficient, and strong. The second reason is that the economy part (specifically the Stokhaned system) is in conflict with left-wing and right-wing, so it may not satisfy some left-wings. Mixing values from Xeroism makes it more ideal for left-wings to live in.

**Why is it called Baliearchism and not Imperial Bastionism?** - The name Imperial Bastionism makes it obvious that it simpathizes Imperialist ideals, which can result in conflict between political parties and possible insecurities against the state. Additionally, Imperial Bastionism is already a term that was meant to show up in the idealimpero for a while, but was scrapped because it gave the wrong idea that it would want to expand its empire, while the actual term was used to declare that the Bastionist state will be ruled by a king, queen, or an emperor. The term Imperial Bastionism can be used on Baliearchists in terms of theorical practise (what it would imply to someone new to the term), but it can be used on Autokratorists on the original practise (what it was meant for).

**Does the Emperor rule over an Autokratorist state because he's superior or because he's the best representative of the people?** - Bastionism on its own abolished hierarchy by letting citizens talk to anyone else like a friend, beliving that it's one of the ways that the human species became unpure and worthy to be succeded. That means that the Autokratorist Emperor must be the best representative of its people while not beliving he deserves to be treated like a superior figure because of his power.

**Is the Vanguard party still kept after the revolution or will it be abolished and the power will be given to anyone else? If the power is given to someone else, who will it be?** - After the revolution, the Vanguard party is still kept ruling during the Ortharchist period and the Baliearchist period of the state, and it can be either preserved or abolished in the Autokratorist period of the state. The new emperor will be chosen based on Democratic vote, with the critera based on **efficiency in ruling over the Kazter during the Ortharchist period of the state** and **its contribution in serving the national military**. In the case that the emperor cannot be choosen, the Vanguard party will choose to either give the power to the citizens directly, or rule until the death of the whole Vanguard party. The only exception on the second option (of ruling until death arrives on each member of the Vanguard party) is if the citizens vote for the abdication of power, therefore bringing the state into some kind of **Anarcho-Bastionist** phase, which abolishes all contact with external states or empires.

**How will restaurants work in a Bastionist state?** - Restaurants are kept, but the payment can be done through either external currencies which will have a certain value set by the Vanguard party or emperor, or have the payment be done through a **certification key** given by the Vanguard party or emperor, which you can get by calling by phone and have a digital copy, or go to the Vanguard party or emperor yourself (reccomended to whoever lives close to their residence) and have a physical copy. In case you want a physical copy but you don't live near the residence, you can request a digital copy that you can print yourself. The certification key will be shown to the waiter once the meal ends and it will lose value depending on what the meal was. The same certification key can be used for anything that isn't the base services like healthcare or housing.
